## Responsiveness during AI work

- Reduce repeated rendering while an AI or Agent response streams, including completed answers, Settings, toolbars, and status controls.
- Batch small text and thinking fragments while showing the first fragment immediately and preserving tool, completion, and cancellation boundaries.
- Defer code syntax highlighting until the response finishes, and avoid rebuilding unchanged session metadata for token or terminal-output events.

## Workflow reliability

- Preserve Chat drafts, pending attachments, queued saves, and tool checkpoints across overlapping actions and conversation changes.
- Keep canceled requests from starting after provider setup and preserve their cancellation state until they finish.
- Retain retry and approval behavior when reopening interrupted conversations.
- Fix stale runbook history responses and overlapping schedule edits, saves, and history updates.
- Apply one deadline to the complete scheduled command sequence and stop remaining work when it expires.

## Terminal privacy and output

- Recognize additional multiline and nested shell forms when classifying commands and protecting sensitive output.
- Improve plain-text capture of terminal cancellation controls and fragmented escape sequences.

These changes retain existing conversation archives and require no database migration.
